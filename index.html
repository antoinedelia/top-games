My Favorite Games of All Time

Here are the games that shaped my gaming life and why I love them.

Hades

An addictive roguelike dungeon crawler where you battle your way out of the underworld.

Roguelike Action Indie

The combat is fluid, the story is engaging, and every run feels rewarding.

Celeste

A precision platformer with a touching story about overcoming inner struggles.

Platformer Indie Challenging

Every jump and dash teaches you patience and rewards perseverance.

Undertale

An innovative RPG where your choices and mercy define the outcome.

RPG Story-Driven Indie

Its charm and humor, combined with a unique combat system, make it unforgettable.

Hollow Knight

A beautiful metroidvania full of challenging combat and atmospheric exploration.

Metroidvania Adventure Indie

The depth of its world and combat make every corner worth exploring.

Nine Sols

A hack-and-slash action game with stunning visuals and fluid combat mechanics.

Action Indie Hack & Slash

The intensity and speed of the combat keep me coming back for more.

Balatro

A quirky indie adventure that blends humor, puzzles, and unique mechanics.

Adventure Puzzle Indie

Its charm and originality make it a delightful and memorable experience.

Return of the Obra Dinn

A mystery adventure where deduction is key to solving the fate of a ship's crew.

Mystery Puzzle Indie

The art style and detective gameplay are unlike anything else.

Clair Obscur: Expedition 33

An atmospheric indie adventure that explores mystery and the unknown.

Adventure Exploration Indie

The ambiance and exploration make it deeply immersive and unique.

Inscryption

A dark card-based game that blends deck-building with horror and narrative twists.

Card Game Horror Indie

Its creativity and tension keep me on edge, making it a thrilling experience.
