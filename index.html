Antoine's Top Games of All Time 🎮

A personal journey through the worlds that shaped me

#1 Hollow Knight (2017)

Hollow Knight is not just a game—it’s an experience. The atmosphere, the music, the world design… every part of it feels hand-crafted. I love how it rewards curiosity and skill, while keeping the story subtle but powerful.

#2 Celeste (2018)

Celeste hit me emotionally in a way very few games have. The controls are tight, the platforming is brutal but fair, and the narrative about overcoming inner struggles is one of the most relatable I’ve ever seen in a game.
